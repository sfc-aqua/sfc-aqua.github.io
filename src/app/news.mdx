## News

Rodney Van Meter delivered a keynote at the 2026 Workshop on Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computer System Architecture in Daejeon, Korea, presenting recent directions in large-scale fault-tolerant quantum computer architecture. Naphan Benchasattabuse also gave an invited talk on distributed qLDPC architectures. (2026/08/29)

AQUA researchers presented new work at IEEE QCE 2025 (Quantum Week) across quantum networking, distributed quantum computing, entanglement distillation, quantum architecture, and quantum algorithms. Amin Taherkhani and collaborators received the 2nd Place Best Paper Award for Automatic Configuration Protocols for Optical Quantum Networks. (2025/09)

AQUA presented multiple papers at IEEE QCE 2024, including work on all-photonic quantum repeaters, heterogeneous quantum networks, satellite quantum links, Bell-state-analyzer switching networks, fault-tolerant qRAM, and quantum network control. (2024/09)
